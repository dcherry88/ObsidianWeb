# Tags Demo

Tags come from two places:

1. **Frontmatter**: this note has `tags: [guide, tags, demo]`.
2. **Inline**: like #inline-tag, #project/alpha (nested), or #status/draft in the middle of a sentence.

Open the **Tags** panel: it lists this note's tags and every tag in the vault with counts. Click one to list matching notes, or click a `#tag` pill in the page.

Numbers and headings are not tags: issue #123 and the `#` above are ignored.
